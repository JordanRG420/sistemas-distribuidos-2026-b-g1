## 2. My Individual Contribution

- I wrote the **Document Purpose** section of the Preliminary Design Review (PDR), establishing the objectives, intended use, and value of the document for the development team and stakeholders.
- I documented the **Background and Need** section, describing the current challenges faced by small and medium-sized retail stores when managing customers, inventory, and sales using manual or disconnected tools.
- I identified and explained the main business problems, including inventory inaccuracies, dispersed information, manual calculation errors, lack of traceability, limited reporting capabilities, and insufficient security controls.
- I defined the **Business Need** and the objectives of the proposed solution, highlighting the importance of centralizing information, automating processes, improving security, and supporting decision-making.
- I developed the **Project Scope** section by specifying the functionalities included in the first version of the system, such as customer management, product and inventory management, sales processing, reporting, and JWT-based security.
- I documented the functionalities explicitly excluded from the project's first phase, including payment gateway integration, electronic invoicing, native mobile applications, multi-branch support, advanced supplier management, and business intelligence features.
